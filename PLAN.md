# Milestone 1: language-conditioned simulated pick and place
A two-revolute-joint MuJoCo tabletop robot with rendered 80×80 RGB and randomized three pucks and three drop zones. Position actuators and known IK translate learned Cartesian increments into motion. A simplified proximity-based grasp latch follows the wrist; not contact-rich grasping. Policy receives RGB, command text, sensed wrist XY, and grasp bit only. Expert has object poses; source only.

Frozen tests: train 6 of 9 color-object/zone combinations; hold out diagonal red→yellow, green→purple, blue→cyan. SHA mixed scene namespaces train/dev/in-domain/test; scene layouts held apart. Two held-out phrase templates share token vocabulary; this is a closed six-color grammar, not open instruction following. Train language-to-color lexical head independently from geometry. Learn RGB color segmentation from simulator-rendered pixels, use color-weighted pixel centroids and fixed camera calibration, and regress BC continuous wrist increments and open/close signal from named object/destination geometry. Three stages trained, no inference use of color IDs other than predicted text/RGB labels or positions outside camera, and no expert action at inference. Low-level joint servo is not itself the model.

Evaluation: true simulator closed-loop trajectories, expert sanity benchmark, matched tasks on identical scenes, executed target fails if wrong puck, wrong zone or timeout. Chance/stochastic controls: shuffled instructions choose a different instruction in same scene (report requested and supplied-goal scores), no-language uses fixed default red→yellow, vision ablation replaces RGB with a fixed training-scene image while retaining real wrist feedback. Compare 24 fresh scenes × all three held-out compositions and all six seen compositions on separate 12 scenes. Expert ≤56 actions. Track reach/grasp/place, steps, task policy ms and environment step/render ms; CPU only. Ablations intervention tests, not separate optimized model classes. Record all outcomes (including no grasp). Frozen test means no choosing best configuration on test. If first frozen run fails, report and fix on dev with separate test namespace. Initial demonstrations and first runnable checkpoint must be deliverable without paid services.
