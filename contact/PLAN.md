# First physical pick-and-place branch

Do not change previous static or motion experiment files. New MuJoCo environment: 2R planar arm, vertical slide, opposed articulated fingers, gravity, three free objects and three visual pads. Objects never use mocap, welded equalities, reset-after-lift, or robot-driven pose assignments after episode initialization. Fingers transfer load via contact and friction. Only the privileged state expert is evaluated, not a learned policy. The open-finger control uses the same expert pose/phase trajectory but the fingers do not close, to demonstrate lift is contact-dependent. Audit XML and source for no attachment.

Development on scratch scenes only. Before confirmation freeze the simulator/expert, scenes/config and analysis. Confirmation: 24 new layouts × 3 assignments (same color pairs withheld in prior project) × expert and no-squeeze control. Randomize initial layout, cylinder radii/heights/friction and color tint; make instructions exist but do not claim expert language inference. Track successful sustained lift, opposed fingertip contacts, carry distance, supported placement, final release, object disturbance, and simulation cost. A success needs object actually raised at least 7 cm, contacted on both sides during lift, carried at least 9 cm, placed within 7.5 cm of pad, fingers open/no remaining fingertip contact, object on table, stable after settle and other objects not moved more than 4 cm. Record all episodes and the control failures. Geometric simplicity, full-state expert, simulated rigid bodies. No sim-to-real or RL claim.
